### 💰 [Eniac Discount Strategy Analysis](https://github.com/Sreemalathi/eniac-discount-strategy-analysis)
**Python · Pandas · Jupyter**

- Analyzed 226,904 e-commerce orders to investigate the relationship between discounting, revenue, and product performance. 
- Built a data-quality pipeline addressing pricing errors, duplicates, missing values, and inconsistent product classifications, resulting in     40,985 analysis-ready orders. 
- Calculated discount depth, seasonality, revenue relationships, and product categories to derive data-driven business recommendations. 

### 🌎 [GoExplore — Market Expansion Analysis](https://github.com/Sreemalathi/GoExplore_analytics_dashboard)
**BigQuery · Looker Studio**

- Analyzed 42 months of sales data (Jan 2015–Jul 2018) covering 562 retailers across 21 countries using Google BigQuery. 
- Built an interactive 4-page Looker Studio dashboard covering retailer, product, performance, and market expansion KPIs. 
- Analyzed 4 potential expansion markets using GDP, population, and Gini Index indicators to support market prioritization.
